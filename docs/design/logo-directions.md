# PlateOS Logo Directions

Three directions were explored in `logo-directions.svg`. Plate Prompt was
selected on 2026-08-31 and refined into the production asset at
`frontend/public/logo.svg`.

## A — Calibrated dish

A top-down plate reduced to two precise arcs and one emerald target notch. It
communicates measured intake without the generic fork-and-knife symbol. This is
the quietest and most legible option at favicon size.

## B — Portion P

A geometric `P` monogram whose counter is a plate and whose emerald quadrant
reads as a portion. It gives PlateOS a stronger proprietary mark and works well
next to the full wordmark. This is the strongest general recommendation.

## C — Plate prompt

A shallow dish paired with a command prompt cursor. It makes the "OS" concept
explicit and feels more technical, but is less immediately recognizable as a
nutrition product. This is the highest-risk, most distinctive direction.

**Selected.** The final mark removes the large concept-board proportions in
favor of a shallow zinc dish and a centered emerald `>_` prompt that survives
favicon sizes. Matching PWA and Apple touch icons are generated by
`scripts/generate_icons.py`.

## Shared constraints

- Must remain recognizable at 16 px and 32 px.
- Must work in one color for masks and monochrome favicons.
- Emerald is a state/accent color, not the full mark.
- No fork, knife, leaf, heart, apple, or bar-chart collage.
- The final mark needs matching 192 px, 512 px, and Apple touch icon exports.
